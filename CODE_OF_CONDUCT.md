# Code of conduct

Payload Palette is committed to a welcoming, technically rigorous community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and patient in reviews and discussions.
- Critique ideas and changes, not people.
- Welcome questions and make technical decisions transparent.
- Respect privacy, attribution, licenses, and responsible disclosure.
- Accept correction and help repair harm when a discussion goes wrong.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, publication of private information, and sustained disruption are not tolerated in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through the contact method shown on their GitHub profile. Maintainers may edit or remove content and temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical, with decisions based on safety, context, and proportionality.
